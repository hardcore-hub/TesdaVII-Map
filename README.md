Map User Guide
- 🗺️ Interactive Map – Explore TESDA offices and locations across Central Visayas.
- 📍 Location Markers – Click a marker to view office information and location details.
- 🔍 Zoom & Navigate – Zoom in/out and move around the map to explore different areas.
- 🏢 Office Information – View details of TESDA Provincial Offices and Regional Office.
- 📱 Mobile-Friendly – Accessible using desktop, tablet, or mobile devices.
- 🧭 Navigation – Click the location or direction link to navigate through Google Maps.
